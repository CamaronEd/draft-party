Step 1 — Find your league ID
If you use ESPN: open your league in a browser. The URL looks like:


https://fantasy.espn.com/football/league?leagueId=123456789
                                                  ^^^^^^^^^ this number
If you use Sleeper: open your draft. The URL looks like:


https://sleeper.com/draft/nfl/1234567890123456789
                              ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ this number
(You can paste the whole URL — it extracts the ID itself.)

Step 2 — Edit config.json
Open C:\Users\calua\draft-party\config.json.

For Sleeper, change these two things:


  "source": "sleeper",
  "sleeper": {
    "draft_id": "1234567890123456789",
That's it — Sleeper needs no login.

For ESPN, change these:


  "source": "espn",
  "espn": {
    "league_id": "123456789",
    "season": 2026,
If your ESPN league is private (most are), you also need two cookies:

Log into fantasy.espn.com in Chrome or Edge
Press F12 → Application tab → left sidebar: Cookies → https://fantasy.espn.com
Find espn_s2 — copy the long value → paste into "espn_s2"
Find SWID — copy it including the { } braces → paste into "swid"
Step 3 — Test that it connects (do this today, not on draft day)

cd C:\Users\calua\draft-party
python app.py --replay
--replay shows picks that already exist. Watch the terminal:

Good: you see your real team names and Watching ESPN draft / Watching SLEEPER draft
Bad — 401 error: your ESPN cookies are wrong or expired, redo Step 2
Nothing happens: that's normal if your draft hasn't happened yet — it just means there are no picks to replay. The absence of errors is the pass condition.
Press Ctrl+C to stop.

Step 4 — The night before your draft

python prewarm.py --top 300
Do not skip this. It takes a while (maybe 30–60 min) and you can walk away. Without it, roughly two thirds of picks show a stat card instead of a video.

When it finishes it prints something like 287/300 players have a playable clip. Anyone missing just gets a stat card, which looks fine.

Optional, an hour before the draft — fills in anyone that came back empty:


python prewarm.py --top 300 --missing
Step 5 — Draft day

cd C:\Users\calua\draft-party
python app.py
Leave that terminal window open and alone. Then:

Open a browser on the TV to http://localhost:8000
Click TAP TO START once
That single click is what allows sound. After it, don't touch anything — it runs itself for the whole draft.

To use a different device as the TV (tablet, laptop, Fire Stick browser): run ipconfig, find your IPv4 address, and open http://THAT-IP:8000 on that device instead.

If something goes wrong mid-draft
Problem	Fix
A clip is bad or boring	press →
Too loud	press M
Want to see the last pick again	press R
Screen froze / went blank	refresh the browser, click TAP TO START again — it reconnects and keeps going
